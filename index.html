About

More than what meets the eye.

I'm a digital artist and 3D character creator drawn to atmosphere, distinctive characters, and the stories hidden beneath the surface.

My work moves between cinematic renders, original character designs, custom 3D creations, comics, and fictional worlds still finding their shape. Some ideas begin with a character. Others start with a single image, a particular mood, or a story that refuses to leave my head.

I care about the details that make a creation feel real - the lighting that changes a scene, the expression that reveals a personality, and the little imperfections that give a character their own presence.

Golfche Space is where these things come together. A home for finished artwork, ongoing experiments, original stories, and whatever comes next.

Not everything here belongs to the same world. That's the point.
